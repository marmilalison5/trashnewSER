SKILL BALANCE

  Twister Kick, Thunder Strike, Dust Cloud, Soul Gasher, Soul Burn,
  Hurricane Blades, Sonic Blast, Provocation.

- Removed the Max HP cut from normal class skills:
  Shake Boxer, Lemures, Ghastly Tempest, Demon Spear Dance, Blood Storm,
  White Hole, Condemn.
