# Ernesh Mobile X patch notes - Tokyo maintenance 2026-11-17 (live APK 613 / data v85). Player-facing only; technical = "Game Optimization".
NEW: CHAT EMOJI AND GIF
- Tap the smile in the chat box: hundreds of emoji in 6 groups, tap one to add it to your message.
- GIF tab: search GIFs (Powered by GIPHY) and send one with a single tap.
- GIFs show in the Chat box only (no head bubble). One GIF per minute - the GIF tab shows the cooldown.

NEW: ACTION BUTTON
- New ACTION button in the button row: hold it to pick a dance or emote (/budots, /yawning, /dance ...), tap it to do the last one again.
- Only the moves your character can do are listed. You can move or hide it in Edit HUD.
- Kangnam dances now play their music.

MUSIC
- New login music, a normal map playlist, and war music for the war events (Club War, Tyranny, School Wars, Capture the Flag, Royal Rumble, Practicing Yard).
- Songs play in your game language when that version exists.

LANGUAGE
- Tagalog was removed (English is used instead).
- Monster names stay in English in every language; broken monster names fixed.

SERVER SELECT
- Shows how many players are online.
- Channels show signal bars and Smooth / Busy / Full.

CHANNELS
- Channel 2 is closed for now. Please use Channel 1 or Channel 0 (Free PK).

EVENTS
- Royal Rumble now takes place in the Practicing Yard: everyone starts and revives in the middle, and there are no exits (the event ends by itself).
- War events can be joined from every channel.

PETS
- Pet skill icons moved under the Force PK button (you can move them in Edit HUD).
- Your pet now teleports with you when Auto Leveling jumps on the same map.
- The pet skill "Pick up all items" is disabled and no longer sold at NPCs.

ITEMS
- Fedora hats have no stats for now and are no longer costumes. Fedoras that were merged into gear are back in your bag.
- Fixed: the Archer freebie set (male) was invisible.
