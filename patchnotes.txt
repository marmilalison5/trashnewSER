76
NEW LOGIN SCREEN
- New look for Login, Server Select and Character Select.

NEW HUD DESIGN
- New HP / MP / SP / EXP / CP panel, Level / Reborn display and weapon slot.
- New minimap with map name, time, your X Y position and phone battery.
- New skill buttons, attack button, joystick, Force PK button and target display.
- New chat window with tabs (All, Whisper, Party, Club, League, System).
- Cleaner names over players and monsters (guild name keeps its background).
- New players start with a ready-made HUD layout (you can still change yours in Edit HUD).

NEW WINDOWS
- New Menu, Add Home, System (General / Video / Sound / Game), Language, Performance and Macro windows.
- New pop-up windows for all messages.

NEW: SOUND EFFECTS
- Sounds when a window opens and when you pick a tab.
- Volume: Menu > System > Sound > Sound Effects.

COMBAT
- Critical hits now work. Your critical chance comes from your items, buffs and skills.
- Ranking critical bonus: Top 1 +6%, Top 2 +5%, Top 3 +3%.
- Critical chance and crushing blow chance are capped at 50%.
- Critical hits and crushing blows deal x1.5 damage. Critical damage numbers are RED.
- New Critical row in the Character window.

EXTREME CLASS
- Extreme Fist can now use ALL skills.
- Skills no longer need arrows, talismans or bullets unless you use a bow, spear or gun.

NEW ITEMS
- Emoticon cards: Patotoya, Yawa, Kupal Ka Ba and oooohaha - an image with sound over your head.

MAPS AND EVENTS
- MarketPlace: new Safe NPC (Locker and Club Locker).
- Royal Rumble: joining the event is fixed.
- Mining Cave: minimap added, new loading screen, crystals renamed.

OTHERS
- Sharper item and skill icons.
- More languages: Filipino, Malay, Indonesian, Thai, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
- Phone back button and keyboard shortcuts no longer open PC windows.
- Playing with Developer Options ON is not allowed (you will be sent back to login).
- Game Optimization.
