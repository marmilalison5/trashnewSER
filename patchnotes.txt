# Ernesh Mobile X patch notes - Tokyo maintenance 2026-11-20 (live APK 667 / data v91). Player-facing only; technical = "Game Optimization".
OPEN BETA TEST
- Ernesh Mobile X is now in OPEN BETA! Everyone starts fresh: all characters, items and progress were reset. Your account (ID and password) stays.
- New characters start at Reborn 99.
- Players who reached MAX Reborn + MAX Level before the reset are saved for a special reward.

ERNESH NPC
- The ERNESH NPC Accessories tab now sells the Divine and Celestial [VIP] accessories.

NEW INVENTORY
- The Inventory is now full screen with a brand new look.
- Item icons fill their slots, and Use changes to Equip / Unequip / Open by itself.
- Locked bag rows now show a padlock.
- Items now shine in the color of their grade (Rare, Unique, Infinity and Epic).

NEW BATTLE PASS
- The Battle Pass is now full screen with a brand new look: Rewards and Missions tabs, your level and EXP, and 5 levels per page.
- Claim Normal and Premium rewards right from the list.
- Premium can now be unlocked right inside the Battle Pass (cash unlock coming soon).

SELLING
- Sell items from your Inventory without opening the shop: just stand near an NPC that buys items (a much bigger range than before).
- Buying still needs you to talk to the NPC.

POINT SHOP
- Costume Set boxes now show their real box icon. Tap the icon to see what is inside.
- The Costume Set tab now only lists sets with 4 or more pieces.
- Store items bought for 15 days now really last 15 days (box contents too).

ITEMS
- Costume boxes, costume weapons and wings are now Infinity grade, and the pieces inside a costume box have the same grade as the box.
- Crushing Blow has been removed from all items.
- Fixed: the Reborn Card was named "Rebon Card".
- Fixed: Gundam No2 wings sat too high (now at the same place as the Miracle Wings).
