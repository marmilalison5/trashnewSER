# Ernesh Mobile X patch notes - Tokyo maintenance 2026-11-19 (live APK 651 / data v86). Player-facing only; technical = "Game Optimization".
NEW POINT SHOP
- Brand new Point Shop: tabs for Store, Package, Costume Set, Costume Weapon, Wings, Pet, Vehicle, Misc, VIP Scrolls and Event Items.
- New SEARCH bar at the top: type a name and find the item in every tab at once.
- Bigger character preview: turn your character with the arrows and see the costume, weapon or wings before you buy.
- New sounds for a successful or failed purchase.

PACKAGES
- New Igris Package (7 or 15 days) for Diamonds.
- Tap an item in "Package Contents" to see it: boxes open the View Box window (tap each piece for its details), other items show their description.
- Cash top-up is coming soon. For now everything is bought with Diamonds.

COSTUMES
- Every Costume Set in the Point Shop is now 7 days, listed once (no more duplicates).
- All costumes now have the same stats as the Portgas D. Ace costume: HP +1000, Damage +500, Magic +100 on Body, Pants, Gloves and Shoes.
- All costume boxes have a new treasure chest icon.
- Costume weapons, accessories and wings have new icons with a glowing frame in your bag.
- Costume weapons in the Point Shop: 300 Diamonds.

WINGS AND VEHICLES
- Wings have their own tab, with Male and Female versions. New: Gundam No2 [Wing] (300 Diamonds).
- All vehicles are now in the Vehicle tab (300 Diamonds).
- Fixed: Gundam No2 costume looked broken / spiky.
- Fixed: Elleon and Sky bows were held the wrong way.

PARTY AND GUILD
- Parties now hold up to 30 players.
- Guilds at the highest rank can now have up to 500 members.

EVENTS
- Club War is now EVERY DAY at 9:00 PM (1 hour).
- Tyranny towers are much stronger: capturing one now takes real teamwork.

PK AND COMBAT
- Force PK targets players only (towers and monsters are ignored while it is on).
- Turning Force PK off now clears a locked target.
- Channel 0 (Free PK): everyone outside safe maps is a red name to other schools, and there is no PK penalty on Channel 0.
- New option: hide your own skill effects (in the same place as hiding other players' skill effects).
- Skills: Crushing Blow removed from the class skills.
