# Ernesh Mobile X patch notes - Tokyo maintenance 2026-11-10 (app 333 / data v73). Rules: player-facing only; technical = "Game Optimization".
NEW: MINING
- Quarrying is now unlocked at level 1 for every class (one Quarrying skill per class).
- Quarrying now deals damage to the crystal.
- AUTO now mines: it uses the Quarrying skill on your skill tray, follows its cooldown and stays on the same crystal.
  Move the joystick to stop.
- One crystal, one player: the first player to hit a crystal owns it, and only that player gets its loot.
  In PK maps, a Pull or Push on the owner frees the crystal.
- Quarrying cooldown is 3 seconds; attack speed buffs cannot make it faster.
- Shorter Quarrying range, so your character no longer stands inside the crystal.
- The green auto-mining button is removed (use AUTO).

NEW: ADD HOME SCREEN
- Menu > ADD HOME SCREEN.
- Tap an icon to move it OUT to the row beside the chat (HOME) or back INTO the Menu (MENU).
- Up to 8 Menu icons in the row. The chat row buttons can be moved into the Menu too.

NEW: JOYSTICK CHOICE (HUD EDIT)
- FIX JOYSTICK: the joystick is always shown in one place (move and resize it in HUD EDIT).
- HIDE JOYSTICK: the joystick appears wherever you touch inside its border (same as before).

NEW: GAMEPLAY GUIDE
- Menu > System > General > GAMEPLAY GUIDE.
- Available in English, Tagalog, Malay, Thai and Chinese; it follows your language setting.

LANGUAGE
- The whole mobile HUD and the System window now follow your language setting.

SKILLS (BETA)
- All learned skills were reset to level 1 and the skill points you spent on upgrades were given back.
- VIP skills are open at level 1. Use skill points to upgrade them.
- New characters start with level 1 skills.
- Fixed buffs failing with "required avoid rate" and weapon requirement messages.
- Speed: only Light Speed, Focused Acceleration, Haste and Massive Haste add movement speed.
  Brawler, Swordsman, Extreme and Shaman: 90. Gunner and Archer: 95.
- Every class has exactly one Pull and one Push skill (normal skills, 90% chance).
  The target now slides, no more walking animation.
- Restored missing skill effects.
- Max HP cut skills (Blood Storm, Lemures, White Hole, Condemn, Shake Boxer, Ghastly Tempest,
  VIP 300G Shaman) now lower Max HP by 99% and can no longer kill in one hit.
- Fixed the game closing when opening the Extreme skill Spirit Shock / Taichi Stream in the skill window.

PVP AND DUEL
- Duel: the loser is no longer killed; the duel ends with a WIN.

GUILD
- Guild dismissal now takes 5 seconds.
- After leaving a guild you can join another one after 5 seconds.
- Fixed the guild icon turning black for the uploader after logging out.

CHARACTERS
- Extreme can now use the gender change.
- New characters appear in the character list right away.
- Players are now visible from as far as their name shows.

ITEMS AND SHOPS
- Item Point Shop updated.
- Item updates.
- VIP skill scrolls, the Grand Master Set and the Sky Weapon no longer drop from monsters.
- Removed unused skill scrolls from shops and drops.
- Costumes: no more HP/MP/SP regen, costumes no longer drop from monsters,
  costume +10000 HP is now +5000 HP, and every costume says "Costume" in its description.
