ERNESH MOBILE X - PATCH NOTES
Tokyo Maintenance - November 22, 2026 (Live APK 709 / Game Data 96)

NEW: ERNESH BOSS EVENT
- Daily event at 10:00 PM, immediately after Club War (delayed if Club War runs late).
- An event card appears for all players. Tap JOIN to register.
- After 5 minutes, registered players are automatically teleported to the Boss Map.
- 288 Bosses spawn across the entire map, including hills. Their locations change every event, and they move around independently.
- Event duration: 1 HOUR. Remaining time appears below the minimap as BOSS EVENT mm:ss.
- Every boss kill drops gold:
  Reflector & Giant Hawk: approximately 100K Gold each.
  Other bosses: approximately 30K Gold each.
- Auto Skill and Auto Attack are disabled inside the Boss Event.
- When all bosses are defeated or the event timer expires, all players are automatically sent back.

NEW: E-ROOM HOLDER TITLE
- The guild holding the E-Room after Club War receives the E-ROOM Holder title for every guild member.
- Bonus: +5% Critical while equipped.
- The title is removed when the next Club War begins.

AUTO LEVELING & NEW MAP
- New leveling map: DEATH PRISON.
- Auto Leveling now takes players to Death Prison.
- New Level 500 monsters: BOY PRESO, BOY TABA, BOY MASO.
- Fast respawn, bonus EXP, and small Gold drops on every kill.
- Other leveling maps return to their normal 50-second respawn.
- World Boss respawn schedule: Giant Hawk, Blaze Angel Mia, Lucian, Red Hair Warrior - respawn every 8 HOURS.

EVENT SCHEDULE
- Club War: Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Friday & Saturday - 9:00 PM (1 hour)
- Royal Rumble: 8:00 PM on Club War days
- Royal Rumble: Tuesday & Thursday - 9:00 PM
- Ernesh Boss Event: Daily - 10:00 PM

SKILL ADJUSTMENTS
- Pull Skills: All classes now pull targets at the same speed.
- Push Skills: All classes now push targets the same distance.
- Removed Slow, Numb, and Burn effects from several skills.

SETTINGS & GAME OPTIMIZATION
- Hide Skill: Changed to one dropdown under System > Game: OFF / OWN / OTHERS / BOTH.
- New Renderer Options: System > General > Performance > Renderer
  DXVK - Default renderer.
  RUST - New graphics engine (test).
- IMPORTANT: Close and reopen the game after changing the renderer to apply the setting.
- If RUST fails to start twice, the game automatically switches back to DXVK.

FIXES
- Fixed broken-looking vehicle, weapon, and effect textures.
- Restored the Club E-Room main computer beside its NPC.
- Fixed game freezes at the intro video on some phones.
- Fixed the text cursor position in the Macro window.
