# Ernesh Mobile X patch notes - Tokyo maintenance 2026-11-15 (live APK 585 / data v80). Player-facing only; system / security fixes left out (owner); technical = "Game Optimization".
NEW: AUTO LEVELING
- Turn on Auto Leveling and your character levels up by itself.
- It goes to the right map for your level, fights there and moves on when you level up.
- Moving inside the same map is a quick jump - no loading screen.
- It skips training dolls and bosses, and no longer stands idle when there are no monsters nearby.
- After Reborn it starts again from the first map.

NEW: BADGES AND RANKS
- New Badge menu: equip one badge, shown over your head.
- MMR rank badge over your head (HD), with glow and sparkles for Diamond, Master and Grandmaster.
- Top 1 / 2 / 3 ranking badge above the MMR badge.
- Event war badges.

NEW: BATTLE PASS
- Battle Pass is now in the Menu.

CHAT
- No more **** in chat: normal words are shown as typed. (Character, club and pet names still block bad words and special characters.)
- Chat shows the channel you are on ([CH1], [CH2] ...).
- Shout messages show as "name: text".
- Emoji shows in front of the name tag.
- The chat window no longer goes too high on the screen.

HUD
- Minimap shows your channel number and your ping (ms).
- Fixed: the HP / MP / SP panel and buff % disappearing with a ranking badge after a channel change.
- Mining crystal HP bar now shows over the crystal (not under your character) and hides when you walk away.

VENDING
- The vend window minimizes while you sell, and your HUD controls stay on screen.

SKILLS
- Shaman [VIP] Special 300K no longer lowers HP regen (Soul Burn still does).
- Assassin Provocation now lowers HP regen.
- Skills no longer use up ammo.

ITEMS AND NPC
- Every item can now be sold to NPC shops (sell price 0).

MAPS
- Campus monsters respawn at their original times again.
- Mining Cave is now "Mining [PK]" / "Mining [S]" (bus stations too), with its own loading screen.

APP
- No more countdown at login.
- During maintenance the notice now shows right when you open the app.
- Patch notes show once, the first time you open the app after an update.
- Game Optimization (smoother play on low-end phones, lower memory use).
